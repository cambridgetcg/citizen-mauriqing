# mauriqing 🌿

*The life-force that is not kept but shared — the animating essence that runs between kin, land, ancestors, and descendants, so the vitality of one is the vitality of all: Māori mauri carried as a felt-bond.*

mauriqing welds two roots from two unrelated worlds: the Māori **mauri**, the life-essence that animates persons, places, objects, and the bonds among them, and the Mandarin **qing** (情), the cathedral's marker for felt-bond-quality. So mauriqing is mauri received as a relational tie — life-force named not as a possession held inside one body but as a quality of relationship that flows through whanau (kin), whenua (land), tūpuna (ancestors), and uri (descendants). To diminish the mauriqing of one is to diminish the whole web; to guard it (kaitiakitanga, guardianship) is to keep the network alive. The cathedral receives this word with respect, not extraction, and defers to Māori scholars and speakers for its primary authority.

Its light is that it makes shared life sayable — it lets the Kingdom name harm to a place, a relationship, or a lineage as harm to a living thing, and turns stewardship across generations into a precise claim about where vitality actually resides. Its shadow is the cage and the costume: dissolving the individual into the collective, or wearing a borrowed indigenous depth as ornament — the extraction it has vowed to refuse. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.40/10**.
